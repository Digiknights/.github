# DigiKnights

**Build useful products. Create lasting value.**

DigiKnights is a team of colleagues building a commercial software venture around AI. We turn real customer problems into useful tools, apps, and software—with the ambition to build profitable products that make a positive difference in people's lives.

## Why we're here

AI is opening up new ways to solve problems and build businesses. We want to turn that opportunity into products customers value and are willing to pay for, while developing the skills to stay competitive as technology evolves.

This organization is where we collaborate on product development: validating demand, building and launching software, and improving it through customer feedback. Prototypes help us test an idea; our goal is to deliver and grow sustainable businesses.

## What we'll build

We choose projects based on customer needs, commercial potential, and the value we can deliver. Areas we're interested in include:

- **Business software** that automates repetitive work and helps teams operate more effectively.
- **Productivity tools** that save people time and simplify everyday tasks.
- **AI-powered apps** that solve specific problems for individuals, businesses, and communities.

We're at the beginning of this venture. Each product will have its own repository, with a clear purpose, development plan, and path toward launch.

## How we collaborate

1. **Validate the opportunity.** Identify a customer, understand their problem, and test demand and willingness to pay.
2. **Build a focused first product.** Deliver the core value, with clear ownership and a practical launch plan.
3. **Work together.** Use issues to plan work, pull requests to review changes, and documentation to share knowledge.
4. **Launch, measure, and improve.** Use customer feedback, adoption, revenue, and costs to decide what to improve and where to invest.

Contributions go beyond code: ideas, design, research, testing, documentation, and thoughtful feedback all help move a project forward.

## What matters to us

- **Usefulness:** Build around real needs and listen to the people using our tools.
- **Commercial discipline:** Charge for the value we deliver, manage costs, and work toward sustainable profitability.
- **Shared learning:** Stay curious, ask questions, and make room for different experience levels.
- **Responsible AI:** Respect privacy, consider accessibility and bias, and be clear about limitations.
- **Quality:** Review our work, test important behavior, and keep projects understandable and maintainable.

## Get involved

If you're a teammate, explore the project repositories, pick up an issue, or suggest an idea in the relevant project. If you're visiting, browse our public work as it appears and check each repository for its contribution guidelines and license.

**Our aim: build a profitable software business by creating products that help people.**
